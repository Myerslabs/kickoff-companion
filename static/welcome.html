Welcome

Two steps and the app is ready: a free key from College Football Data, then your team. The rest is optional.

Kickoff Companion is free, from Myers Labs. It runs on your computer and talks to CollegeFootballData.com directly: your key, your team and everything you look at stay here, and nothing is sent to Myers Labs. CFBD's paid plans are paid to CFBD, not to us, and they only unlock more data.

Not ready yet? Go back to the demo

Step 1. Your CFBD key

Every number in the app comes from College Football Data (CFBD). A key is free: sign up with an email address and CFBD mails it to you.

Get a free key See CFBD's plans

A key is already set. To change it, open this page on the server computer itself, so nobody else on the network can swap it.

The key is saved in the .env file on the server computer and never sent back to a browser.

Step 2. Your home team

Your primary team #1. The Season page, the Game program and the Live sheet follow it, and only this team's colors and mascot theme the app. Its conference comes from CFBD.

The list loads once the key is checked.

Step 3. More primary teams optional, up to 4

Teams you follow closely, like a second school in the family. Each one's team page (schedule, results, stats, ranks, roster) loads as soon as a device connects and sits one tap away in the menu. They do not change the app's colors, and the Season, Game program and Live pages stay with your home team.

Step 4. Secondary teams optional

Teams you like to keep an eye on: single schools, a whole conference, or every school in a state. Nothing is loaded for them until a page asks, so they cost almost nothing. They show on the My teams page, in the My teams view of every ranked list, and on the ticker when it is set to My teams.

Step 5. The score ticker

The scrolling scores at the top of the Live sheet, the Game program and the Newspaper.

Change it any time in Settings.
